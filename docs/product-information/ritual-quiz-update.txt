NatureMist ritual finder — implementation update, 24 October 2026

Implemented locally; not deployed. This update supersedes the goal labels and
benefit-based matching copy in the 11 October quiz draft. Existing colour,
reaction, hair-history and strand-check branches remain in effect.

ENTRY POINTS
Hair cleansing, Hair packs, Scalp care, Botanical colour, Skin research,
Body research, and Hair + skin or body.

ADAPTIVE RESEARCH ROUTES
Skin research offers Amla, Reetha, Hibiscus and Bhringraj.
Body research offers Reetha and Shikakai.
Customers choose one ingredient or all relevant ingredients.
Indigo is not offered as a skin/body research ingredient.
Results use the shared botanical-uses.ts references and explicitly describe
what was studied, limitations and a source link. These routes do not produce
face/body application directions or automatic hair-product purchase matches.
Customers can explicitly restart to explore a hair ritual.

COMBINED ROUTE
Choose skin or body research, choose an ingredient, then choose a hair goal.
The ordinary adaptive hair questions determine the hair-product match.
The selected research appears separately below the hair result.

PRODUCT MATCHING
Soapnut/oil-cleansing preference: Reetha.
Traditional Shikakai wash or undecided first wash: Shikakai.
Floral pack or Hibiscus preference: Hibiscus.
Amla or general herbal pack preference: Amla.
Traditional hair/scalp pack: Bhringraj.
Compatible darker-colour method with henna-first step: Indigo, subject to
the existing colour checks. No alternative powder is sold as a colour match.
Two-step choices still ask for a complementary pack or wash preference.
Reasons describe the selected ingredient/role rather than promise shine,
softness or improved manageability from an untested finished powder.

DATA AND NAVIGATION
Live matches require verified Shopify product identity, matching ingredient,
directions, sale availability and an available variant. Missing products
are not silently replaced in personalised recommendations.
Editing any earlier answer clears all dependent answers. Restart clears all.
Research references never grant skin/body product-use eligibility.
