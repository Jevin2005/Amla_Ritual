Information still needed for face/body product directions
Product guide!E7:G11 and Benefits and evidence!F30:G30 / F33:G33 describe
possibilities or traditional associations. Combos!B12:G12 explicitly describes
the face/body duo as a development option. No skin mixing ratio, application
time, frequency, rinse method or product-specific tolerance results are supplied.

To enable actual skin recommendations, record for each intended skin product:
exact species and supplied plant part; face/body/both; preparation quantity and
mixing method; application and rinse method; contact time and frequency;
patch-test directions and restrictions; source of the confirmed instructions.
These values must come from the product owner/supplier, rather than being inferred
from extract studies or invented from generic botanical recipes.
