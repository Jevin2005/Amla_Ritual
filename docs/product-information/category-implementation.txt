Botanical categories implemented locally — 23 October 2026

Shopping categories
Hair packs: Amla, Hibiscus, Bhringraj.
Hair cleansing: Reetha, Shikakai.
Hair colour: Indigo.

Shopify remains the source of shopping classification through custom.ritual_step.
Read-only verification found Pack on the three single pack products and four
pack combos, Cleanse on Reetha/Shikakai/the cleansing duo, and Colour on Indigo.
The loader now accepts Pack as the existing internal Condition key. Previously,
Pack became an empty step and products disappeared from the pack filter.
No Shopify values were changed.

Homepage ingredient explorer
The existing specimen section now has All botanicals, Hair care, Skin research,
Body research and Hair colour controls. It uses the existing botanical artwork.
Skin: Amla, Reetha, Hibiscus and Bhringraj, with the actual study/tradition and
its limitation attached to each card.
Body: Shikakai formulation research and Reetha development inference.
Indigo is excluded from both research views.

Product pages
The six known single products show their current use and an expandable wider
research guide. Sources open separately. Research is not a purchase eligibility
flag, a skin-use instruction or a promised benefit. Product structured data now
distinguishes the current use category instead of labelling every item hair care.
Known Shopify IDs keep evidence attached when a product is renamed. Unknown
products and combos do not inherit the single-ingredient guide by name similarity.

Boundaries
The research records are editorial source summaries in code, not Shopify-managed
fields. Shopping filters still read the Shopify ritual-step field. Quiz question
logic has not been expanded to recommend face/body application. Existing hair
logic now receives the previously unrecognised Pack classification correctly.
No deployment, publication or metaobject deletion was performed.
