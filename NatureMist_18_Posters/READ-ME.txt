NATUREMIST - SIX PRODUCTS / THREE POSTERS EACH

18 individual image designs, supplied in two formats:
  WebP-website: smaller files for website uploads.
  PNG-originals: full-quality original generated images.

Use the numbered files in your three website image spaces:
  01-ingredient  = wide ingredient photograph (left slot)
  02-preparation = preparation photograph (middle slot)
  03-jar         = tall product jar photograph (right slot)

Each product has a different setting:
  Amla: orchard and sunlit vanity
  Reetha: wooden ingredient tray and cream spa stone
  Shikakai: rustic wood and terracotta alcove
  Hibiscus: floral stone and blush courtyard
  Bhringraj: fresh herb harvest and greenhouse
  Indigo: leafy study and blue-grey stone

The supplied Amla jar is the master for the white body, black lid and
200 g label family. The other five labels are adaptations of that template.
All six final jar posters show NET QTY. 200 g.

Images contain no website headings or carousel buttons. Keep these as live
website elements. Select one matching three-image set per product.
Do not stretch the photos. Use their original aspect ratio where possible.
For fixed image boxes, preview the crop on desktop and phone; keep the full
jar and its label visible. The ingredient frames are 7:5; the jar frames are
8:15. Preparation photographs have individual native dimensions listed in
image-index.csv. Select their focal point around the mixing bowl.

The original PNG pixels are unchanged. WebP versions preserve image size
and composition and use quality 88 compression.
