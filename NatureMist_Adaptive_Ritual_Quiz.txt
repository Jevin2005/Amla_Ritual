Question bank and product-routing specification
Updated 22 October 2026

SCOPE AND STATUS
Four starting choices: Hair Care, Skin Care, Both Hair & Skin, and Hair Colour.
Catalogue scope: Amla, Reetha, Shikakai, Hibiscus, Bhringraj and Indigo powders.
This file specifies the revised experience. Updating this file does not update or deploy the website quiz.

Aligned with the current local website catalogue, collection filters and guided-quiz source. The public product URLs could not be fetched during this revision, so current live content and availability have not been independently reverified.

The website currently identifies the five non-colour powders for hair washes or traditional hair packs, and Indigo for hair colour. Its local product descriptions do not establish facial or body use. Skin Care and Both remain visible quiz choices, but must return an honest availability/guidance result until the exact products have confirmed skin-use directions. A category button, ingredient tradition or jar illustration is not proof of an approved use.

CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
Heading: Find your ritual.
Introduction: Explore a botanical ritual for your hair, skin, both, or hair colour.
One question at a time. No preselected answers. Continue stays inactive until a valid answer is selected.
Back preserves valid answers. Editing an earlier answer clears dependent answers and recommendations.
Show Question N and the stage name; do not promise a fixed question count.
Recommend at most two distinct products. Never add a second product unless the customer requests another step.
Use ingredient preference and the product's stated role. Do not promise softness, shine, regrowth, acne relief or a guaranteed colour.

The website's Both filter means a product explicitly has BOTH Hair Care and Skin Care in careAreas. It does not mean every hair powder is suitable for skin.
A separate hair-only product plus a skin-only product may form a two-step plan, but must not be presented as two dual-use products or linked as a matching Both-filter set.
Use "Hair Colour" in displayed copy, matching the site. Accept "color" as a search synonym.

PRODUCT ELIGIBILITY - RESOLVE BEFORE ASKING BRANCH QUESTIONS
Hair candidate:
  Confirmed Hair Care classification, matching Cleanse or Pack role, current hair-use directions and a purchasable matching variant.

Skin candidate:
  Confirmed Skin Care classification AND current directions for the requested application area, with no conflicting restriction.
  "Skin Care" alone does not establish facial use, body use, sensitive-skin suitability or treatment benefits.
  Within this quiz, Amla and Hibiscus are the only proposed skin candidates to review for future activation. Neither is currently confirmed for skin use by the local product copy.
  Reetha, Shikakai, Bhringraj and Indigo must not become skin recommendations merely because they are botanical powders.

Dual-use candidate:
  Meets both hair and skin requirements for the exact same product. Show separate hair and skin directions.

Colour candidate:
  Indigo with confirmed Hair Colour classification, current colour directions and the compatibility checks below.

Live selection:
  Resolve product identity, ingredient, careAreas, directions, restrictions, current variant and stock from the catalogue.
  Conflicting directions override a category tag. Missing or uncertain intended-use information produces guidance, not a guessed recommendation.
  Preview data must never be treated as verified live stock, price or purchase eligibility.
  If an item is unavailable, show that fact. Do not silently substitute a different care role.
  At every eligibility gate, distinguish unconfirmed intended use from confirmed products that are only out of stock. Use the no-stock result for the latter; do not describe their skin use as unconfirmed.
  No skin or dual-use candidates in the current catalogue is an expected outcome, not an error.

ROUTING RULES
1. Everyone: careArea -> check. Active irritation takes priority and ends in pause. Previous reaction -> reactionIngredient -> review. Uncertain quick check -> review. Neither -> selected care branch.
2. Hair wash: Reetha preference -> Reetha; Shikakai preference -> Shikakai. First wash/unsure -> Shikakai as a simple ingredient-based starting option, not a claim of superior mildness.
3. Hair pack: flower -> Hibiscus; fruit/general/unsure -> Amla. A traditional hair-and-scalp pack selected through scalpNeed -> Bhringraj.
4. Hair history: unknown -> review. Treated -> treatmentType; unknown treatment -> review. Known history -> continue.
5. Hair-only routine: one/unsure -> one candidate; two -> complementary addon question. Skip retains one. Packs and washes are separate steps using each pack's directions, not an invented mixed recipe.
6. Skin: check eligibility before ingredient questions. No eligible product -> "Skin use is not yet confirmed" with no recommendation. Eligible -> skinArea -> filter again -> skinNeed -> skinChecks.
7. Skin comparison: return up to two eligible alternatives, clearly labelled "Choose one to start." Do not call them a routine, imply mixing, or automatically add both to the bag.
8. Both: no eligible skin option -> bothFallback. Otherwise bothPlan. Shared/unsure -> skinArea with dual-use filter -> sharedNeed -> hairHistory -> treatmentType when relevant -> skinChecks -> strand when relevant.
9. Both separate: bothPlan -> skinArea -> hairGoal and its hair-selection follow-ups -> hairHistory -> treatmentType when relevant -> skinNeed (one item only) -> skinChecks -> strand when relevant. Do not ask hair-only addon questions; the skin step is the requested second step.
10. Both separate may use a hair-only product and a skin-only product. Label their intended areas separately. If the same verified product is selected for both, show one product card with two separately directed uses.
11. Any stop/review answer on a combined plan prevents a complete "ready" result. Do not carry a recommendation into a pause or review result. An explicit bothFallback choice is the only automatic transition to hair-only planning.
12. Non-colour strand: passed -> continue; notyet -> conditional; changed/unsure -> review. Natural untreated brown/black hair does not require this extra quiz question; ordinary label-directed checks still apply.
13. Skin check: passed -> continue; notyet -> conditional; reaction -> pause. On Both, complete all relevant skin and hair checks. If either remains uncompleted, the overall result is conditional.
14. Colour: lighter/copper -> no matching colour product in this range; unsure target/base -> guidance. A lightened base or dye/bleach/chemical/uncertain history -> compatibility review.
15. Supported colour history -> henna. No/unsure -> method guidance. Ready -> colourChecks. Passed -> Indigo-stage candidate; notyet -> conditional; reaction -> pause; unwanted shade -> review.
16. Indigo belongs only to Hair Colour. Do not add it to skin, dual-use, cleansing or general hair-pack results. Amla and Hibiscus do not replace henna.
17. Irritation, previous reactions and unsupported use always outrank product preferences. No percentages, "perfect match", diagnosis, treatment promises or allergy-safe substitutions.
18. Refresh availability before purchase. Do not present a partial two-step result as a complete plan. Explain any missing step and let the customer edit.
19. Read price, pack size, stock and directions from current product data. The local single-powder catalogue uses 200 g. Do not retain the old 250 g discrepancy as a current fact, or hardcode price, dose, timing or frequency.
20. Shikakai and Reetha are hair cleansers. Amla, Hibiscus and Bhringraj are traditional hair packs. Use Pack rather than claiming proven conditioning, softness or shine.

EXACT PATH SUMMARY
Hair:
  careArea -> check -> hairGoal -> washNeed / packNeed / scalpNeed / hairStep
  -> hairHistory -> treatmentType if treated -> routine
  -> packAddon / washAddon if requested -> strand if light/treated -> result.
  Scalp symptoms, uncertain history and unresolved step selection exit as described above.

Skin:
  careArea -> check -> skin eligibility gate.
  No eligible product -> guidance.
  Eligible -> skinArea -> area eligibility gate -> skinNeed -> skinChecks -> result.

Both:
  careArea -> check -> eligibility gate.
  No skin candidate -> bothFallback -> explicit hair-only path or guidance.
  Eligible -> bothPlan.
  Shared -> skinArea -> dual-use eligibility gate -> sharedNeed -> hairHistory
    -> treatmentType if treated -> skinChecks -> strand if light/treated -> result.
  Separate -> skinArea -> skin eligibility gate -> hairGoal and hair-selection follow-ups
    -> hairHistory -> treatmentType if treated -> skinNeed
    -> skinChecks -> strand if light/treated -> result.
  A failed area gate returns guidance; a failed shared dual-use gate offers bothFallback.

Hair Colour:
  careArea -> check -> colourTarget -> colourBase -> colourHistory
  -> henna -> colourChecks -> result, subject to the early exits above.
  Do not ask skinArea, hair routine or addon questions on this path.

RESULT COPY AND ACTIONS
Candidate:
  Title: Your suggested starting ritual.
  Care: Hair Care / Skin Care / Hair & Skin / Hair Colour.
  Product: current product name, image and intended role.
  Why it fits: a short explanation tied to the customer's ingredient and step choices.
  Directions: link to the exact product and the relevant use.
  Actions: View product directions; Edit my answers.
  Purchase actions appear only for eligible, available variants.

Shared Both:
  Title: One botanical, two separately directed uses.
  One product card.
  Hair use: its approved hair step and directions.
  Skin use: its confirmed face/body directions.
  Do not reuse prepared paste between areas or invent a shared recipe.

Separate Both:
  Title: Your hair and skin starting plan.
  Step 1: selected hair product, labelled Hair use.
  Step 2: selected skin product, labelled Skin use.
  These are separate uses, not a mixture or a required same-session sequence.

Conditional:
  Title: Check before starting.
  Name the exact unfinished checks and the products/areas they concern.
  Show directions and Edit my answers. No start-now instruction or automatic add-to-cart.

No confirmed skin use:
  Title: Skin use is not yet confirmed.
  Copy: We do not currently have a matching powder with confirmed directions for your selected skin use. Explore its stated uses, or choose another care path.
  Actions: View product-use guidance; Edit my answers.
  For Both, offer the explicit hair-only continuation through bothFallback.

No stock:
  Title: Your selected ritual is unavailable right now.
  Explain the unavailable item or step. Never substitute an unsupported skin or colour product.

Pause / review / guidance / no match:
  Explain the unresolved point in plain language. No personalised product recommendations.
  Allow editing and appropriate product-information or contact links.
  General browsing must be labelled as browsing, not as a quiz match.
