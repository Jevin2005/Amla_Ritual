Question bank and product-routing specification
Prepared 11 October 2026

Scope: Amla, Reetha, Shikakai, Hibiscus, Bhringraj and Indigo powders. This is proposed shopping logic grounded in the published product descriptions, not a diagnosis or a claim of a perfect outcome. It has not been deployed to the website.

ROUTING AND RESULT RULES
1. Resolve exclusions and guidance routes before ranking products. A preference score must never override an irritation, reaction or unsupported colour route.

2. Cleanse → oil/buildup: Reetha. Cleanse → manageability, first wash or not sure: Shikakai. The last two are modest starting suggestions based on product roles, not proof that one powder is clinically superior.

3. Softness + shine → dry/rough or manageability: Hibiscus. Shine, general conditioning or not sure: Amla.

4. Scalp care → oil/buildup: Reetha. General hair-and-scalp conditioning or an explicitly requested mask: Bhringraj. Uncertain users choose wash versus mask next.

5. Persistent scalp symptoms and sudden/patchy loss end in professional guidance. Do not recommend Bhringraj as a treatment for dandruff or hair loss.

6. One product or uncertain routine: return one main powder. Two steps: ask what the additional step should do. Never add a second item merely to increase basket value.

7. For a cleanser plus mask: dry/manageability → Hibiscus; shine/general conditioning → Amla; scalp conditioning → Bhringraj. Keep the cleanser if the customer skips.

8. For a mask plus cleanser: oil/buildup → Reetha; cleanse/manageability → Shikakai. Keep the mask if the customer skips.

9. Treat two products as separate care steps, not an invented mixed recipe. When a pre-wash mask and a cleanser are both selected, show the mask first and the wash second; use the pack instructions for each.

10. Light/grey or treated hair triggers strand guidance. Unknown history or unexpected strand results ends in review. An uncompleted test shows a conditional candidate with a directions link, not an instruction to begin.

11. Botanical Colour is a separate path. Lighter/blonde and red/copper goals have no verified match within this six-powder catalogue. Unknown desired or starting colour goes to guidance.

12. Existing non-henna dye, bleach, chemical treatment or unknown colour history goes to compatibility review before automatic colour selection.

13. Indigo can appear only for a deeper-tone/grey-darkening goal with known starting colour and a suitable henna-first plan. Never promise that Indigo alone makes grey or blonde hair black. Henna is not a verified SKU in this six-product scope.

14. Before an Indigo full-application suggestion, ask about label-directed patch and strand checks. Not done: conditional candidate. Reaction: stop. Unwanted shade: review. Passed: colour-stage candidate, not a guaranteed shade or safety certification.

15. No known reactions is self-reported, not evidence of allergy safety. Previous reactions end in ingredient review; do not recommend an alternative as allergy-safe.

16. The result contains at most two products, an answer-specific explanation, the current product-directions link and Edit answers. No match percentages, invented discounts, review counts or treatment promises.

17. Live implementation: use verified SKU IDs, ingredient lists, compatibility flags and availability. Suppress unavailable products; if no suitable verified alternative remains, show no available match rather than silently switching roles.

18. Start with no preselected answers. Keep Continue inactive until an answer is selected. Back preserves answers. Changing an earlier answer clears all dependent later answers. Do not keep a product from a previous branch.

19. Do not promise five questions for every branch: routine care usually takes five; colour, treatment and second-step follow-ups add only relevant questions. Display Question N and a stage label, or count actual steps after branching.

20. Copy quantities, timing and frequency only from the current approved label. The public site currently has some 250 g text while recent artwork work uses 200 g; this quiz intentionally omits weight, price and dose until the live SKU and pack are reconciled.

21. Classify Shikakai as a cleanser in the quiz. The live collection displays a conflicting colour-ritual tag, while the detailed product page describes cleansing.
