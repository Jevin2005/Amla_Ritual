# Shery.js native gooey effect and debug panel

The ingredient poster uses the original Shery.js 1.0.0-beta.0.1.8 browser distribution, including its built-in ControlKit Gooey Panel and Debug Panel. NatureMist supplies iframe isolation, visibility scheduling, image selection and slideshow integration. The library's shaders and panel construction are unchanged.

Shery.js uses its compatible Three.js 0.155.x and GSAP dependencies. Their original distribution notices are retained under `/vendor/shery/`. The ControlKit README, including its MIT license (Copyright 2013–2014 Henryk Wollik), is available at `/vendor/shery/ControlKit-NOTICE.md`; the Three.js license is at `/vendor/shery/Three-LICENSE.txt`. Shery's bundled notices are at `/vendor/shery/Shery.js.LICENSE.txt`.
